🌳 CITY BUILD

🪙 250 🚗 0 🛣️ 2
Draw a road from an existing road. Make as many bends as you like!
HOW TO PLAY
✏️ Draw roads with unlimited bends.
🌊 Connect roads to the beach exit.
🚧 Toll gates earn coins from cars.
⛽ Fuel stations cost 80 coins.
🌉 Bridges cross roads without joining their traffic.
⬆️ Select a road and upgrade it.
Tip: Road connections are drawn without circular end blobs.
